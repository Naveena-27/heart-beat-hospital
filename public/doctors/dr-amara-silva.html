Dr. Amara Silva

MBBS, MD (Cardiology), FRCP — Consultant Cardiologist

Dr. Silva leads the Heart Center at Heart Beat Hospital, specialising in interventional cardiology and the long-term management of heart disease. Known for taking the time to explain every diagnosis in plain language, she has treated thousands of patients across Colombo and continues to combine advanced cardiac imaging with a calm, reassuring bedside manner.

  • Coronary angioplasty and stenting
  • Heart failure and hypertension management
  • Preventive cardiology and risk screening
  • Post-surgical cardiac rehabilitation

Available: Mon, Wed, Fri — 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM
