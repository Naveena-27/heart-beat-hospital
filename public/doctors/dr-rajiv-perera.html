Dr. Rajiv Perera

MBBS, MD (Neurology) — Consultant Neurologist

Dr. Perera heads the Neuro Center, where he diagnoses and treats conditions affecting the brain, spine and nervous system. His practice is grounded in careful, methodical diagnosis — he's known for ordering exactly the tests a patient needs, not more — and for guiding patients and families through complex neurological conditions with patience.

  • Stroke diagnosis and management
  • Epilepsy and seizure disorders
  • Migraine and chronic headache treatment
  • Nerve conduction studies and EEG
